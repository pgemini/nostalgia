Kancha

कंचे

Aim your marble and flick! Knock out as many kanchas as you can from the circle.

Marbles
?
Click to aim and flick your marble. Try to knock opponent marbles out of the ring!
🪨

Lagori

लगोरी / सात पत्थर

Throw the ball, knock the seven stones, and rebuild the pile before you get hit!

Seven Stones
?
Aim and throw the ball to topple stones. Then stack them back before the timer runs out!
👟

Stapu

स्टापू / कित्थ-कित्थ

Hop through the squares with perfect timing! Don't step on the lines.

Hopscotch
?
Toss the stone and hop through numbered squares. Land perfectly to score points!
🪀

Lattu

लट्टू

Spin the top and keep it going! Master the art of the perfect spin.

Top Spinning
?
Hold and release to spin the lattu. Control the power for the longest spin!
